What we keep about you, and who sees it

What we store

Your directory entry starts from what you gave the club when you applied: your name, email, role, company, city and program. ID numbers, birth dates, allergies and phone numbers from the application are never copied in. Everything else is what you add yourself: what you can help with, what you're looking for, interests, LinkedIn, a bio, a photo, and a WhatsApp number if you want one shown.

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We also keep the events you sign up for, whether you attended, what you post on the board, and the day you last visited, so the leaders can run the club.

We don't use cookies for tracking, we don't run analytics, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone. Partners get no access to the members list.

Who can see it

Only signed-in members see the directory, and the club's leaders decide which groups of members see each other. Your email and WhatsApp number are shown only if you tick the box for each on your profile. You can also hide yourself from the directory altogether. Other members see your name on events you've signed up for, unless you've hidden yourself. The club's leaders can see the full members list, including emails and attendance, to manage access and events.

Where it lives

The data is kept in a Google Sheet managed by the club's leaders, and the site reads it through Google Apps Script. Your browser keeps a sign-in token and a copy of the directory so pages open quickly; signing out removes them.

Changing or deleting your data

You can edit your profile, change your photo or hide yourself at any time on the site. To have your data removed entirely, ask one of the club's leaders on WhatsApp, and we'll delete it.
