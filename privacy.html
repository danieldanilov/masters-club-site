Privacy

What we keep about you, and who sees it

What we store

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We use the email only to check that you're on the members list. Everything else in your profile (role, company, industry, what you can help with, what you're looking for, LinkedIn, bio, and a WhatsApp number if you add one) is what you type in yourself.

We don't use cookies for tracking, we don't run analytics, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Who can see it

Only signed-in members see the directory, and the club's leaders decide which groups of members see each other. Your email address is never shown to other members. Your WhatsApp number is shown only if you tick "Show a WhatsApp button on my profile". The club's leaders can see the full members list, including emails, to manage access.

Where it lives

The data is kept in a Google Sheet managed by the club's leaders, and the site reads it through Google Apps Script. Signing in keeps a session token in your browser for up to 30 days; signing out removes it.

Changing or deleting your data

You can edit your profile at any time on the site. To have your profile removed entirely, ask one of the club's leaders on WhatsApp, and we'll delete it.
